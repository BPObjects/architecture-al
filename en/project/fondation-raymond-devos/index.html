Culture & Recreation

Fondation Raymond Devos

Located in the Chevreuse Valley, the Fondation Raymond Devos seeks to revitalize its activities. The former home of the “clown” has now been converted into a museum.

The addition is intended to accommodate shows, performances, or exhibitions for 80 people, as well as additional storage.

The building aims to be as discreet as possible, blending into the verdant landscape as fully as it can.
