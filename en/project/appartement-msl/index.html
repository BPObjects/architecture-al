MSL Apartment

Complete reconfiguration of an apartment in the 18th arrondissement of Paris. The project was designed to maximize the living spaces by creating transparency between the sleeping area and the living/kitchen area.

Particular attention was paid to the atmosphere and lighting, with a fully connected smart-home system.
