Studio

Paris 16th arr. · since 2009

Since 2009, Architecture-AL has designed housing, offices, industrial and cultural facilities, retail, and hotels, in France and abroad: from a Paris apartment to the aeronautics hub in Istres, from an abbey in Normandy to a hotel in French Guiana.

We lead projects from concept design through construction, for private clients, companies, and fellow architects. Our work connects construction and digital technology: BIM models, 3D renderings, virtual reality, and BPO, the configurator for building components and buildings that we develop.

Antoine Lacronique — Architect & BIM Expert

An architect who graduated from the École Spéciale d’Architecture in Paris (1996), Antoine Lacronique has spent nearly 30 years exploring the convergence of physical space and digital innovation.

A career at the forefront

As early as his thesis project, he created the @irnet C.A.F.E. (Communication Access For Everybody) concept, a pioneering vision of digital access for all, for Radio Nova and Novapress (alongside J.F. Bizot).

He then founded the practice impactArchitecture (1996–2006), where he collaborated with prestigious names such as Marc Barani and Rena Dumas, and worked on large-scale projects for the Stade de France, Unibail, and the POPB.

The digital pivot: The GDL & BIM revolution

Expert in and representative for the French-speaking countries and the US of GDL technology for Graphisoft, Antoine became the bridge between the building industry and digital architecture. Founder of Abvent Industrie and of the Cat-e (electronic catalog) platform, he revolutionized architectural specification by creating smart 3D catalog and configurator solutions for global leaders such as:

  • Technology & Innovation: Google, Trimble.
  • Industry & Design: Saint-Gobain, Isover, Technal, Velux, Grohe, Terreal.

Architecture_AL: Expertise that serves the project

Drawing on this dual expertise — command of parametric tools and the rigor of construction — he founded Architecture_AL in 2009.

Today, he works closely with his clients, taking a resolutely contemporary and personalized approach. His studio designs projects in which high-level digital expertise takes a back seat to close attention to the client and a passionate architecture focused on use and aesthetics.

Cyril Chênebeau (partner)

Cyril Chênebeau, born in 1971, is an architect in Nice (Alpes-Maritimes) and holds the DPLG (French state-certified architecture degree). He graduated from the École d’architecture Paris Tolbiac in 1997 with highest honors from a jury chaired by Alexandre Chemetoff (2000 Grand Prix de l’urbanisme, France’s national urban planning award).

Starting in 2001, at Marc Barani’s firm, he notably led the design work, then the construction administration, for the multimodal transit hub and tramway maintenance facility in Nice, a project that received the Équerre d’Argent prize in 2008.

In 2008, he went into private practice and has since been developing his own projects across diverse project types and programs: single-family and multifamily housing, office buildings, rehabilitations, interior build-outs, public facilities, and infrastructure and transportation projects.

In 2015, he received two awards for the rehabilitation of a rural house in Sclos de Contes (Alpes-Maritimes): the Rehabilitation Prize as part of the ArchiCOTE competition and the MPF – Patrimoine & Architecture Prize awarded by the Maisons Paysannes de France association.

In 2021 and 2022, he received, in consecutive years, the ArchiCOTE competition’s Rehabilitation Prize for the creation of an apartment built around a courtyard in downtown Nice, and the Single-Family House Prize for a house designed for a contemporary art collector in Le Rouret (Alpes-Maritimes).

Several of his most recent completed projects are in housing: in 2020, he completed a major rehabilitation of 96 housing units in Cap-d’Ail (Alpes-Maritimes), then, the following year, 8 affordable housing units built on a steep site near Antibes (Alpes-Maritimes). In 2022, he completed 11 senior housing units in the Saint-Barthélémy neighborhood of Nice, as well as 2 wood-construction cabins on stilts for use as tourist accommodations, on the edge of Mercantour National Park, for the Alpes-Maritimes departmental council.

The firm Cyril Chênebeau Architectes continues to grow, working on diverse projects at various scales, such as the construction of a restaurant pavilion on the grounds of the Musée Fernand Léger in Biot (Alpes-Maritimes), a project for horticultural production greenhouses for the City of Nice’s new parks department in the Nice Méridia – Grand Parc neighborhood, in association with the firm MILK, and the creation of a parking structure topped by an “overlook” space at the foot of the old village of Aspremont, in the hinterland of Nice.

Our tools

The studio is equipped with ArchiCAD and Lumion licenses, tools we have mastered at an expert level, and several Mac and PC workstations. It also develops its own software:

  • BPO

    Configures building components and buildings to your dimensions, in your finishes, and exports them to IFC, DAE, or OBJ, ready to place in ArchiCAD, Revit, or SketchUp.

    bpo.archi →

  • CALQUE

    Online design review: the IFC model and PDF drawings are shared with the team and the client and annotated remotely; a Products tab lets the client approve 3D objects and product data sheets, prices included.

    calque.bpo.archi →

  • MOULINETTE

    Simplifies heavy 3D models (OBJ, DAE, 3DS) while preserving small parts, detects and fixes defects on import, and exports lightweight objects, all the way to a .glb ready for Calque.

  • NUAGE

    Offline point cloud viewer: surveys of more than one hundred million points, imported as .xyz, .pts, or .csv, explored in real time, with height clipping.

  • PDFBPO

    The studio’s PDF editor: compress, correct text, sign, reorder, extract or merge pages, and print.
